About Us

Animal Preservation Agency

Snow leopards are large cats native to the mountain ranges of Central and South Asia. They are known for their beautiful, thick fur and long tails, which help them navigate the rugged terrain of their habitat. Snow leopards are elusive and solitary animals, making them difficult to study in the wild.
